# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-04

First public release of Contour3D, by Mikhail Bovt.

- Reference-guided Blender authoring with seven editable construction sources.
- Native/evaluated inspection, sections, UV diagnostics and scoped thickness samples.
- Versioned geometry, shader and dependency contracts; protected regions and rollback.
- Local deformation, native controls, mesh repair and bounded parameter fitting.
- Real render packets and built-in Codex imagegen provenance/native-transfer workflow.
- Bundled local 3D workbench with region selection and revision-bound edit requests.
- Static GLB export/reimport checks for scale, bounds and triangle budgets.
- Public installation, support, privacy and terms documentation; reproducible release ZIP.

The plugin helps avoid modeling mistakes and improve results through references and checks. It does not guarantee dramatic quality gains; results also depend on the GPT model and source material.
