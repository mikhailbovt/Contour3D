# Vehicle form and authoring

Use for vehicle exteriors, interiors and game-oriented vehicle assets. Establish intended visual style, actual reference coverage and delivery scope. A model intended for Cyberpunk still needs coherent proportions and body construction before ornamental cyberpunk detail.

## Primary form

Use wheel centers, wheelbase, track, ground plane, roof envelope and principal body stations as datums. Cross-check a known dimension against aligned side/front/top observations. Reference uncertainty is not permission to invent hidden mechanics as established fact.

Resolve cabin proportions, windshield rake, roof crown, shoulder and wheel arches before small lamps, bolts and decals. Read recognizable body lines across separate panels; positionally aligned panel edges may still have incompatible tangents. A body that looks correct from a three-quarter view can have a wrong roof/cabin silhouette in orthographic views.

Use controllable sources for broad panels. Preserve their crown and interface boundaries, then fit glazing, seals, trim and aperture returns. Model a rounded formed edge with its intended thickness and radius. Avoid a dense high resolution skin whose subtle shape is controlled only by scattered constants in unrelated scripts.

## Assembly and interior

Separate moving panels and their meaningful pivot/attachment references. Check a door, bonnet or boot through its useful envelope when movement is in scope. Keep glass, seals and hardware attached to the correct part. An auxiliary Blender armature is not necessarily the game's vehicle rig.

Cabin shape must fit the exterior shell. Prioritize seat volume, controls, dashboard structure and clearance around the driver view. Tiny fasteners cannot compensate for oversized trim, floating upholstery, unexplained overlaps or a missing attachment.

Diagnose nonplanar caps and tightly rounded details. Inspect triangle areas and triangulation on sun visors, lids and formed recesses rather than trusting manifold counts alone. Preserve unaffected UVs, weights and material slots during a local repair.

## Density and game delivery

Allocate detail by actual view and a declared target budget. Keep the authoring model and delivery LODs separate. Underfloor ribs, headlamp fluting and upholstery can dominate triangles; choose their geometry/bake representation deliberately for the intended close-up and runtime.

Do not infer a triangle limit from a different game, renderer or whole-vehicle budget. Check silhouette and material appearance across LOD transitions. Repeated details and exported material splits influence cost alongside triangle count.

For Cyberpunk delivery, verify the agreed game materials, rig mapping, collision/shadow/damage needs and asset registration through the actual pipeline. An imported GLB is an interchange result. Say which runtime work remains instead of labeling it game ready.

## Checks and next edits

Use matched neutral side/front/top views, a grazing roof/body view, panel-interface crops and the relevant cabin view. Test a primary surface change without displacing a protected seam and an independent secondary detail change. Confirm the native result can be reopened and edited without the plugin.

Good: recognizable silhouette, coherent interfaces, useful shape sources and documented delivery scope. Bad: technical reports with no inspection of proportions, or millions of triangles presented as proof of professional finish.

A whole car is a diagnostic development case, not a universal benchmark. Validate the same procedures on non-vehicle shapes before broad quality claims. Existing branded/user-owned assets and their references stay outside the distributable plugin unless redistribution is established.
